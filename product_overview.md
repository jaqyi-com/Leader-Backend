# Doott: Product Overview

> **An AI-powered B2B Sales Intelligence platform that gives sales teams instant access to verified company data, decision-maker contacts, emails, phone numbers, and an always-on AI assistant — all in one place.**

Doott is a focused, data-first platform built specifically for the Indian B2B market. It eliminates the hours sales teams spend hunting for prospect information by surfacing verified, structured intelligence on companies and people through a clean, fast interface powered by a large proprietary database and an AI chatbot.

---

## 🚀 Live Features

### 1. 🏢 Companies Database
Search and explore a curated database of Indian B2B companies.
- Filter by industry, category, and geography.
- View company profiles with key business details.
- Instantly export filtered lists to CSV for your sales team.

### 2. 👥 People Database
Find verified decision-makers at target companies.
- Search by name, job title, company, or location.
- Access structured contact records including seniority and role.
- Identify the right person to reach — CTOs, founders, VPs, and more.

### 3. 📧 Emails
Look up verified business email addresses for prospects.
- Search for emails tied to specific companies or individuals.
- Reduce bounce rates with pre-verified contact data.
- Copy and export emails ready for outreach campaigns.

### 4. 📞 Numbers
Access verified phone numbers for B2B contacts.
- Retrieve direct-dial and business phone numbers for Indian contacts.
- Paired with contact profiles for immediate action.
- Built for India — local numbers, local context.

### 5. 🔲 Categories
Browse the entire database organized by industry and business category.
- Explore leads by vertical: SaaS, Manufacturing, Finance, Healthcare, and more.
- Quickly drill into a niche to surface the most relevant companies and contacts.
- A powerful starting point for any outbound campaign.

### 6. 💬 Ask Doott (AI Chatbot)
An always-on AI assistant built directly into the platform.
- Ask natural-language questions about companies, industries, or contacts.
- Get instant, intelligent answers powered by a curated knowledge base.
- Available 24/7 — no waiting, no manual lookup needed.
